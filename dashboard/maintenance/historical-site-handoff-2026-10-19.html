A site owner needs the keys

This case shows how to hand a site to its owner. A live page and a way to edit it let the owner keep work moving. Use the site-delivery guide to plan both checks before you launch.

The lesson for the next delivery

A page link alone does not finish the handoff. Check the live page, confirm the owner can use the needed access, and name who takes the next step. Save that person's response with the delivery record. Keep any open fixes visible.
