What changed and why

The personal-brand site build recipe now links its opening to the next delivery task. Its new record table asks for the preview revision, page checks, contact result, domain and mail checks, owner decision and receiving acknowledgment.

Reviewing older delivery proof showed why those details matter. A recipient's reply can show that a site was received. It cannot recover an unknown recipe version or prove checks that were never recorded. The improved recipe tells the next worker to capture those facts as the work happens.
