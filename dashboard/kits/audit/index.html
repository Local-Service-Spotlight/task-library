How the full 20-page audit is built

    
Seven skills run in order. Each one writes to the same evidence ledger, so every claim in the PDF points back to a source anyone can open.

    
    
      
  1. Prove who it is
    Match two independent facts, like company and city, before anything counts.

  2. Collect the proof

  3. Compare with the buyer you want
    Find proof that is missing, and proof that exists but is buried.

  4. Ask the AI tools
    Run real questions in Google, ChatGPT, Gemini and others, and record the sources they cite.

  5. Fix the entity home
    One website that tells Google who you are, with Person data and a Knowledge Graph check.

  6. Turn real work into content
    Produce, Process, Post and Promote from recordings and stories you already have.

  7. Put a dollar a day behind winners
