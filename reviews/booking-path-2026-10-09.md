# Check appointment promises in a contact path

These checks help a team see whether people can book when the page says they can. They help catch gaps before more people visit. They extend the [conversion-path guide](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=create-clear-conversion-path#task-create-clear-conversion-path), which follows a request from a web page to its receiving owner.

## What the actual lesson changes

A reachable booking page is not enough when the offered appointment falls outside the stated promise. The guide now records the check time, time zone, first selectable slot and any mismatch. It also catches contradictory answers and requires the tested booking ID to match the assigned eligible host, saved answers and receipt. The owner's real promise controls the test; there is no universal response-time rule.

The original authorized intake conversation and the existing delivery owner's packet supplied the lesson. They remain private. A reported setup is not a successful booking, staffing proof, delivered notification or accepted result. This maintenance attempt did not submit a booking or message, change host access, buy a plan or take over the existing job.

The guide's additional example is explicitly fictional. A displayed slot mismatch does not by itself identify a staffing, subscription or calendar-sync cause. The existing owner retains that diagnosis and follow-up.
