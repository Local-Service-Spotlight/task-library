# Create Dollar a Day ad creatives from best content

Use your best past content to plan the next small ad test. This guide turns a proven post into a clear draft for the right people. Start with the post, its results and the source file.

**The path:** Post + real results → Chosen source → Placement drafts → Ads owner

**Start when:** Published content has relevant organic evidence and the promotion owner needs tested-source creative drafts.

## Inputs

- [Prepare social clips](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=create-social-content-from-video-clips#task-create-social-content-from-video-clips) or another published asset with source files, use rights and measured results.
- The intended goal, audience, destination and current promotion brief.
- Actual placement requirements, editing tools and any separately approved test budget; [Dollar a Day, small paid tests of proven content](https://blitzmetrics.com/dollar-a-day/).

## Steps

1. Compare the content against the goal it served. Retain date, audience, reach and relevant watch, engagement or lead signals. Choose useful evidence-backed pieces; an old top ranking or a large raw like count alone does not establish a current winner.
2. Check that the source claim, person’s permission, offer and destination are still accurate. Prefer proven useful work under the house greatest-hits method, while keeping any separately approved new experiment clearly labeled as unproven.
3. Write [Goals, Content, Targeting: the result, source material and people served](https://blitzmetrics.com/gct-business-strategy/) for each selected creative. Name the precise result, source-backed message and intended audience. Keep a path to the relevant proof or offer; a generic personal-brand message with no useful next step is not an adequate brief.
4. Adapt the actual source to the agreed placements. Check current aspect ratio, duration, file and caption requirements in the selected platform before exporting. Keep the hook and subject visible, preserve meaning and crop safely; do not assume one vertical file fits every placement.
5. Prepare the ad text, media, destination, source ID and test proposal as one review packet. Use the house small-test method only within the promotion owner’s actual budget and setup. This creative-preparation task does not create ad sets, spend money or call a nominal daily budget risk-free.
6. Preview the complete creative on the intended device sizes, checking captions, safe areas and working destination. Keep media muted at zero volume. Record rejected variants and missing source evidence instead of certifying an unseen placement.
7. Hand off accepted files and the proposed measurement window to the ads owner. Paid launch and later pause/scale decisions use their own authority, actual tracking and current campaign evidence; no universal two-times increase or automatic kill rule is imposed here.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

Quality assurance (QA) means checking the actual result against its agreed requirements. Follow the [Article Guidelines](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/).

- [ ] Each draft traces to a usable source with goal-relevant evidence and current rights.
- [ ] The intended placement, text, captions and destination have a checked preview.
- [ ] The packet separates creative readiness, approved budget and actual ad launch.
- [ ] The exact output, source revision, reviewer evidence and remaining owner action are saved.
- [ ] For any reader-facing output, the short grade-five opening states the reader’s useful outcome and supporting method or proof. The body delivers that promise; a useful authentic visual appears in the first screen. Retain exact text and quoted reviewer evidence.
