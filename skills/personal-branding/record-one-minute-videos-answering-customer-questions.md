# Record one-minute videos answering customer questions

Film one short clip to help your clients. This guide helps you give clear answers in your own words. Start with a real question and a true story that helps explain your point.

**The path:** Real question → Clear answer → Saved take → Checked source handoff

**Start when:** The person has real knowledge to share and the content plan needs new source recordings.

## Inputs

- Actual customer questions from permitted calls, notes or messages, with private details removed.
- The person’s relevant experience, source facts, phone or camera and consent for the recording.
- [Topic Wheel, the map of your offer, topics and real stories](https://blitzmetrics.com/topic-wheel/) coverage plan and an accessible content-library destination with a receiving editor.

## Steps

1. Collect real questions and record their context without exposing private messages or client data. Choose the question because it serves an actual customer need. A proposed AI topic is a suggestion, not evidence that customers asked it.
2. Use the Topic Wheel correctly: WHAT offer at the center, HOW teaching topics in the middle and WHY people/stories outside. Map the selected question to its useful topic; not every short recording has to cover all three parts of the wheel.
3. Prepare a brief prompt and source reminders instead of a fabricated first-person script. Follow the [one-minute video method](https://blitzmetrics.com/one-minute-video-guide/): lead with the point, give a useful answer or true example, and end with an appropriate next step. About a minute is the target, not a reason to cut away needed meaning.
4. Check light, stable framing and the intended microphone before the session. The person records their own answer. During agent QA, keep any playback muted at zero volume; use waveform, captions, transcript or a separate authorized human listening check for sound quality.
5. Record one complete idea per take. Redo a factual mistake or unusable take rather than patching together a misleading quote. A batch can be useful, but its size comes from the real questions and available time, not a forced count of videos.
6. Name and save each real file with question, speaker, date and take identifier in the accepted library. Record rights, source facts, rough transcript and selected/rejected state. Verify that the editor can read the actual file; a pasted inaccessible link is an incomplete handoff.
7. Pass selected takes with exact timestamps and any remaining sound or fact checks to the editor. Keep planned, recorded, usable and published states distinct. The recording itself does not prove later captions, posts or business results.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

Quality assurance (QA) means checking the actual result against its agreed requirements. Follow the [Article Guidelines](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/).

- [ ] Each selected take answers a real scoped question with the speaker’s own supported knowledge.
- [ ] Files, rights, identities and actual usability checks are saved in the accepted library.
- [ ] The editor can access the selected source and sees any still-unverified audio or factual issue.
- [ ] The exact output, source revision, reviewer evidence and remaining owner action are saved.
- [ ] For any reader-facing output, the short grade-five opening states the reader’s useful outcome and supporting method or proof. The body delivers that promise; a useful authentic visual appears in the first screen. Retain exact text and quoted reviewer evidence.
