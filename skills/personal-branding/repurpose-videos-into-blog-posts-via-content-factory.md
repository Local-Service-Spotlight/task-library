# Repurpose videos into blog posts via Content Factory

Make a clear page from a real video. This guide keeps your real voice and proof while making the lesson clear. Start with one checked recording and the reader it should help.

**The path:** Real recording → Checked transcript → Useful article → Reviewed release

**Start when:** A permitted usable recording needs a source-backed article on the person’s maintained site.

## Inputs

- [Record real customer answers](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=record-one-minute-videos-answering-customer-questions#task-record-one-minute-videos-answering-customer-questions) or another accepted video with speaker, date, rights and source file.
- The intended reader, useful result, existing relevant pages and source proof.
- Actual transcription/editor access, maintained publishing source and release owner; [Article Guidelines](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/).

## Steps

1. Check the original file, permission, speaker and intended use. Read any existing article on the topic before drafting; improve the maintained owner or create the appropriate supporting page under the [SEO Tree, the map from proof pages to main topics and offers](https://blitzmetrics.com/seo-tree/) rather than duplicate a competing topic hub.
2. Transcribe with the tool actually available, then compare names, numbers and load-bearing statements with the source. Keep exact timestamps for useful quotes and corrections. A transcript is evidence to check, not permission to invent a more impressive story.
3. Define [Goals, Content, Targeting: the result, source material and people served](https://blitzmetrics.com/gct-business-strategy/) from the actual material and assignment. Draft the short opening at grade five or below, explaining the reader’s situation, useful outcome and supporting method. Use [Review the hook and context](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=step-7-write-hook-and-establish-context#task-step-7-write-hook-and-establish-context) and retain quoted reviewer evidence.
4. Write a clear article with title under 60 characters, concise meta description under 160, useful headings and short active paragraphs. Preserve the person’s authentic first-person account and named credited quotes. Include relevant credibility proof; do not rewrite a lived example into generic advice or a claim the speaker never made.
5. Place meaningful real media or a source-backed diagram with the short opening and embed the source video where permitted and useful. Check captions, source identity and responsive layout silently with volume zero. Do not substitute an empty featured-image box for actual first-screen evidence.
6. Use [entity linking, choosing the right home for a named person or topic](https://blitzmetrics.com/entity-linking/) for correct person, company and method destinations; retain primary proof links where they support a claim. Link the article to its real topic and relevant offer. Set author attribution to the actual author or approved source-derived author, not an arbitrary admin or invented speaker credit.
7. Review the source, meaning, media, links, spelling and actual desktop/mobile presentation. Preserve the existing page type, builder and durable source. Save as draft or publish through the authorized rail, then check the normal public page only if release occurred.
8. Save transcript/source links, exact draft or public revision, review results and remaining actions in the existing content record. Hand real sources and accepted excerpts to the clip task; writing an article does not mean its social derivatives or promotion have run.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

Quality assurance (QA) means checking the actual result against its agreed requirements. Follow the [Article Guidelines](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/).

- [ ] The article preserves the source’s facts, voice, credited proof and useful lesson.
- [ ] The exact opening and full body pass meaning review; first-screen media and links are checked at required sizes.
- [ ] Saved/released states, true author and durable source are recorded accurately.
- [ ] The exact output, source revision, reviewer evidence and remaining owner action are saved.
- [ ] For any reader-facing output, the short grade-five opening states the reader’s useful outcome and supporting method or proof. The body delivers that promise; a useful authentic visual appears in the first screen. Retain exact text and quoted reviewer evidence.

If silence cannot be verified first, use captions, frames, metadata or another silent check.
