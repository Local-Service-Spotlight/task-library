# Get mentioned in publications

Help the right readers learn from your real work. This guide turns a useful fact or story into a clear press pitch. Start with a source you can prove and a writer who covers that topic.

**The path:** Real story + proof → Relevant writer → Specific pitch → Checked coverage

**Start when:** The person has a verifiable story or expert contribution suitable for a publication.

## Inputs

- A source-backed story, useful data or expert response, with permission to share the material.
- A current press kit, accepted name and home site, and a verified publication/writer list.
- Submission rules, deadline and outreach owner; this task’s dedicated canonical article remains unmapped.

## Steps

1. Choose outlets and writers whose actual recent work covers the topic. Read the editorial focus and submission rules. A prestigious name alone does not make the story relevant, and a purchased placement is not independent earned coverage.
2. Build the pitch around a true new fact, useful lesson or timely perspective. Verify numbers, dates, qualifications and client permission. State what the source proves and what remains uncertain; do not create news by inventing a milestone.
3. Prepare a concise response or pitch with the reader value, specific angle, supporting sources and a relevant bio. Keep quotes attributable and in the person’s voice. Use [Article Guidelines](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/) for plain context and proof, without turning the message into a company sales page.
4. Send through the writer’s real accepted channel only under existing authority. Track the exact message, recipient, deadline and reply. If a journalist asks a follow-up, verify the new facts before preparing an answer; speed does not substitute for accuracy.
5. Coordinate any interview or source material with the actual editorial terms, attribution and use rights. Paid promotion is a separate optional task; do not assume an ad can or should target an individual journalist before a pitch.
6. When coverage is reported, inspect the published source for the correct identity, exact quoted meaning, disclosure and link. Prepare a factual correction if needed and keep requested versus accepted changes distinct. A pitch or interview does not establish publication.
7. Add the verified source to the proof inventory with date, independence, permission and exact supported claim. Keep the missing canonical task article as a visible mapping gap; use the current personal-brand method hub for context without calling it this task’s finished recipe.
