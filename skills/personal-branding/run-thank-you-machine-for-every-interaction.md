# Run Thank You Machine for every interaction

Let people know how their help mattered. This guide turns a real act of help into a specific thank-you in the right place. Start with what the person did and whether the story is safe to share.

**The path:** Real helpful act → Specific thanks → Right private/public route → Checked follow-through

**Start when:** A genuine referral, review, introduction, advice or useful act deserves acknowledgment.

## Inputs

- The actual event or interaction, identity and source facts, without unnecessary private details.
- The relationship context, public-use permission and preferred acknowledgment route.
- Recording or writing tools and actual send, release or spend authority where relevant.

## Steps

1. Read the real interaction and name what deserves thanks. Record the person, act and why it mattered. A routine automated event or inferred relationship is not a reason to fabricate gratitude; make the acknowledgment specific enough to be true.
2. Choose the appropriate route for the person and situation. Some thanks belong in a private note; others may become public content with the right permission. An interaction does not automatically consent to a public story, tag, testimonial or ad.
3. Prepare a short unscripted video or a clear note naming the person, what they did and its real effect. Preserve the speaker’s voice and an authentic human detail. Do not inflate a favor into an endorsement, measured result or durable partnership.
4. If content reuse fits the agreed scope, use [Content Factory: Produce, Process, Post and Promote](https://blitzmetrics.com/content-factory/) to make only the useful approved outputs. Keep the core story and proof intact. A video does not require a blog, testimonial, email and ad in every case, especially when the source is private.
5. Publish or send through the existing authority and correct destination. Verify any social tag identifies the right person and record the actual delivered/live state. A tag does not guarantee that they see or share the post.
6. Consider [Dollar a Day, small paid tests of proven content](https://blitzmetrics.com/dollar-a-day/) only for an appropriate public asset with useful organic evidence, use rights, tracking and a separately approved test budget. Gratitude does not require paid amplification, and a nominal daily budget is not a guarantee of outcome or zero risk.
7. Add an appropriate thank-you check to the existing post-project process and retain the event, draft, release and response. A weekly review may be useful when assigned; an installed skill does not create a schedule. Learn from actual responses without measuring success by forced public posts.
