# Generate backlinks and entity mentions

Help readers find your work from places they already use. This guide helps you earn links by doing useful work. Start with the links you have and the ones your readers need.

**The path:** Existing mentions → Useful target list → Real contribution → Checked credit

**Start when:** The site has useful proof and the team needs a bounded plan to earn or recover relevant editorial credits.

## Inputs

- The current backlink and mention inventory, home URL and approved identity facts.
- Actual audience needs, relevant work to cite and a list of suitable publications, shows, events or associations.
- Outreach owner and scope, source/reuse rights, and the existing proof record.

## Steps

1. Record the current baseline and its coverage: known source URLs, referring domains, mention text, date and destination. A tool export may be incomplete; unavailable or unindexed sources are not automatically absent from the web.
2. Prioritize destinations by audience fit, editorial relevance and actual relationships. Prefer a missing credit for real work, a useful expert contribution or a relevant member listing over a generic domain-authority target. Do not claim to know which source Google trusts most.
3. Use the relevant podcast, speaking, press or collaboration recipe to prepare a specific contribution. Supply facts, original experience and source proof. An exchange intended chiefly to manipulate rankings is not the editorial relationship this task teaches.
4. Prepare a concise credit or correction request to the actual source owner when it would help the reader. Link the accepted name to the verified home where editorially suitable. Requesting a link does not guarantee the host will add it, and no send is implied without authority.
5. Keep paid placements, sponsorships and advertising separate from independent editorial coverage. Follow [Google’s link spam rules](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/essentials/spam-policies) for paid or exchanged links. Any paid distribution plan belongs to its own authorized promotion task, not an automatic journalist-targeting step.
6. After a placement is claimed, open the actual source and verify the intended person, text and working destination. Record a mention with no link as that, and a sponsored item as sponsored. Keep a dated screenshot or reference sufficient to review the observation.
7. Update the existing proof inventory and baseline, including requests that remain pending or were declined. Report changes and limits through [Metrics, Analysis, Action: what happened, why and what to do](https://blitzmetrics.com/maa/). A completed research pass is not the same as newly earned coverage; preserve the actual outcome.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example. This is not a client result or proof of a completed run.**

A fictional engineer finds an event page that credits her talk but omits her website. She prepares a short correction request with the right URL. The host keeps the credit but declines the link. The inventory still records a real speaking mention; it does not mark a new backlink or treat the host’s answer as a failed identity.
