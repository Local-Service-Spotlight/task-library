# Keep speaker bureau profile bookable

Give event hosts the facts they need to book your talk. This guide keeps your speaker page and booking profile in line with one approved kit. Start with your current talks, fees and real proof.

**The path:** Approved kit → Exact profile fields → Real proof → Checked booking path

**Start when:** The speaker’s public booking surfaces are missing facts or differ from the current approved speaker kit.

## Inputs

- The speaker’s current approved kit with source revision, bio, talks, public fees, permissions and contact route.
- Actual bureau/profile access and the personal-site speaking page, plus their release owners.
- Real headshot, stage photos, talk video, attributed testimonials and confirmed public event dates.

## Steps

1. Read the kit and current public pages before touching fields. Compare dated source revisions and flag conflicting current facts to the kit owner. The maintained approved kit governs this pass; do not replace a newer authorized change with an older source merely because it is in git.
2. Confirm the person’s current positioning, operating company and approved public contact details. Preserve former companies as accurate history where relevant. Never publish a private street address, personal phone or unpublished fee just to fill a directory field.
3. Use exactly the fee policy the speaker has approved for public use. Enter actual U.S., international, virtual and travel terms where applicable; if fees are not approved for publication, keep the decision with the owner. Do not claim a universal bureau ranking penalty for an inquiry-only price.
4. Load each approved talk with its title, format, intended audience and useful takeaways. Write clear grade-five context explaining the audience’s benefit, backed by the real program. Use first person on the personal site and the bureau’s required voice in its profile.
5. Add real relevant proof: a headshot, stage image or talk video and attributed testimonials with supported event context. Check media and rights silently. Link the actual speaking page and its inquiry path, not a homepage that hides the booking details.
6. Enter only confirmed, permitted public calendar events. Private holds and tentative invitations remain internal or explicitly tentative where appropriate. The source’s earlier three-date target never permits invented future appearances or a claim that an empty calendar means inactivity.
7. After authorized updates, compare the public bureau URL and speaking page with the approved kit. Check the first-screen value, program, proof, fees policy and contact destination. A form submission test may send a message, so use the existing test authority and verify its destination.
8. Save the changed fields, exact public URLs, kit revision and pending owner actions. Review when programs, fees or events change. Future speaking footage goes to the content owner after the event actually occurs.
