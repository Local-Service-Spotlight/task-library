# Tag the Right Person in a Thank-You Post

A tag can help the right person find your thank-you post. This guide helps your team avoid tagging the wrong person or sharing a private story. Start with a checked post. Finish with the right tag and proof of the post’s state.

**The path:** Checked public post → confirm profile → check the saved post.

**Use this when:** public posting is within scope and naming or tagging the person is appropriate for this story.

## Inputs

- The checked media, caption and source note.
- The authorized account, intended channel and verified profile for the person.
- The person’s stated preferences and any limits on public sharing.

## First-run prompt

> Check this thank-you post and the person’s profile. Add the correct tag if the story can be shared. Carry out the already authorized posting step, then check the saved post. If posting is not in scope, save the exact draft and missing decision.

## Steps

1. Confirm that the content is intended for public use. Remove private details and respect a request not to be named or tagged; omit a tag if no suitable profile is confirmed.
2. Open the profile from a known link or verify its name, photo and relevant role against the project source. Do not select a similar name from search without evidence.
3. Use the platform’s actual tag or mention control on the intended posting account. A typed name is not proof that a clickable tag exists.
4. Read the full caption with the tag. Keep the thank-you specific and free of sales pressure. Do not promise that tagging will notify everyone, force a reshare or reach the person’s audience.
5. Preview the correct media and captions. Check that the tag does not cover a face or important text and that the destination account is the intended one.
6. Publish when already authorized. Otherwise save the concrete draft and the one decision needed. Never label a draft as posted.
7. For a posted item, reopen its normal permalink from the correct account. Check the visible media, caption and profile destination. Save the permalink, time and actual state; a platform’s notification delivery may remain unknown.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] Public sharing and the correct posting account are established.
- [ ] The person’s profile and tag destination are verified or the tag is deliberately omitted.
- [ ] The caption keeps the real meaning and excludes private facts.
- [ ] The exact saved post or draft is checked.
- [ ] No reshare, endorsement, audience reach or notification delivery is inferred from a tag.
