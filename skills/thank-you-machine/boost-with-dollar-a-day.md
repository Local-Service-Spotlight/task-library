# Test a Thank-You Post With a Small Ad

A small ad can test whether a good post helps your business. This guide helps you use a thank-you post with a clear goal and a set budget. Start with a checked post. Finish with the test result and a choice to stop or keep going.

**The path:** Checked post and budget → small test → measured next choice.

**Use this when:** the post is suitable for paid use and the owner has named a goal, audience, total budget and decision date.

## Inputs

- The checked live post, its organic results if available and rights for paid use.
- The goal and success measure, allowed audience, ad account, budget, dates and authorized actions.
- Working measurement for the chosen result; a view goal and a sales goal require different proof.

## First-run prompt

> Use this post and budget to prepare the Dollar-a-Day test. Check current account limits, calculate total possible spend across all ad sets, and show the settings. Run only the spending actions already authorized. At the review date, use actual results to propose the next step.

## Steps

1. Write the goal, source post and audience in plain words using the [Goals, Content, and Targeting brief](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=gct-goals-content-targeting#task-gct-goals-content-targeting). Choose a useful measure and target before launch; gratitude or a tag is not evidence of sales.
2. Read the current Dollar-a-Day method and the actual platform’s budget rules. The initial method is $1 per day for seven days where supported. Daily budgets can be averages; this is not a universal hard $7 cap.
3. Count every planned campaign or ad set and its currency, dates and spending limit. If a minimum exceeds the authorized amount, reduce scope where supported or return the exact decision needed. Do not silently increase the budget.
4. Use a relevant audience supported by the current account. Do not assume named-person targeting, sensitive attributes, arbitrary interests or access to the recipient’s audience. Confirm the exact post and paid-use rights.
5. Prepare the ad, objective, destination, placements and measurement. Check a preview silently. Save the settings and total budget calculation so the owner can review any new spending decision concretely.
6. Launch only within the authorized account and spend scope. Read back the saved settings and delivery status. Record pending review, rejected or not delivering honestly; save the ad ID and check time.
7. Observe the planned test, but stop or repair a broken destination, consent issue or spend error promptly. At the decision date, compare actual spend and the chosen outcome with the target. Mark sparse or untracked data as inconclusive.
8. Propose stop, revise or a bounded winner test. The maintained winner stage is $30 total over 30 days where applicable; it is not $30 per day and is not automatic approval. Record any authorized next action and verify its saved state.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] The specific post, paid-use rights, goal, audience and account are checked.
- [ ] Budget arithmetic covers all ad sets, dates, currency and actual platform rules.
- [ ] Saved settings and delivery state are verified if launched.
- [ ] The review uses actual measured results and identifies missing data.
- [ ] A next decision is recorded without guaranteed reach, return or automatic scaling.
