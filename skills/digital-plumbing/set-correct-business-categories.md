# Set Correct Business Categories

The wrong business type can confuse people who find you on Google. This guide helps an owner choose the right type for the work they do. Start with the main service your business really sells.

**The path:** Real work → Available categories → Saved choice → Public check.

**Use this when:** a Google Business Profile has inaccurate categories or the business’s actual main work changes.

## Inputs
- The exact Business Profile and current editing access, with its verification state known.
- The owner-backed core business description and real additional services or departments.
- Current primary and additional categories, plus their source evidence and any existing review issue.

## First-run prompt

> Match the actual business to Google’s available categories. Make the authorized minimal category edit, then record whether it is saved, pending or publicly visible. Do not select a false category to mimic a competitor or promise a ranking lift.

## Steps
1. Describe the business as a whole and its main activity. Consider its important revenue service, but do not choose a category for a minor high-priced offering that misrepresents the overall business.
2. Open the exact Business Profile and record current categories. Verify the role supports the edit and read any existing verification or pending-review notice before submitting another change.
3. In Edit profile → Business category, search the current available list. Choose the most specific accurate primary category. If the exact one does not exist, a truthful broader category is valid; you cannot create a custom category.
4. Add only a few appropriate additional categories for genuine business activities. Do not add one for every product, planned service or independently operated neighboring business.
5. Compare the proposed set with the website’s actual service claims. Competitor categories can suggest a question to check, but are not proof of what this business does or what will improve its ranking.
6. Save the authorized change and record the submitted values and time. Category edits may prompt reverification. Preserve that state and use the supported review or verification route rather than repeatedly resubmitting until a badge changes.
7. Read the management state and public profile after the provider processes the edit. Check whether the intended choice is visible; a saved form or elapsed number of days is not acceptance proof.
8. Save the chosen categories, factual reason and review result. If rejected, identify the provider’s stated reason and the exact next action, without changing to an inaccurate category just to get through.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] The primary category accurately describes the real business and is available in Google’s list.
- [ ] Additional categories represent genuine appropriate activities.
- [ ] The change is backed by business facts, not only competitor positions.
- [ ] Submitted, pending, verified and public-visible states have distinct evidence.
- [ ] No ranking, suspension or processing-time guarantee is invented.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example.** Oak Repair installs fences as its core service. The picker offers “Fence contractor,” so that is a more accurate primary choice than a broad contractor label. The owner does not provide landscaping, even though a nearby competitor lists it. That category is left out. The lesson records one submitted primary edit and marks the public result pending until it is actually observed.
