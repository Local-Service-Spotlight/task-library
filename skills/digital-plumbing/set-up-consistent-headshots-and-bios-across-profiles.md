# Set Up Consistent Headshots and Bios Across Profiles

Do your profiles tell the same true story? This guide helps you keep the facts and photos clear. Start with a real photo and a short story you can prove.

**The path:** Verified identity → Approved assets → Profile variants → Public proof.

**Use this when:** a person or business changes its public positioning or finds stale, conflicting or inappropriate profile assets.

## Inputs
- The correct person or business identity, current approved real image, rights and source-based identity confirmation. Do not identify people by comparing faces.
- A canonical factual bio with supported claims and short/long variants suited to the actual audiences.
- The in-scope profile list, field limits and usable editing access. Separate personal profiles from company Pages and service-area business listings.

## First-run prompt

> Update the supplied profile set from its approved image and factual bio. Adapt length and voice to the actual person, business and platform without inventing claims. Record public read-backs and legitimate differences. Preserve evidence HOLDs and private data.

## Steps
1. Separate the person, business and location profiles. Decide which asset each should use: a personal headshot may fit the individual while the company uses its logo. An identical face on every surface is not the definition of correct identity.
2. Confirm the image from an approved source record and publication rights. Use a current real image for the role. Do not infer who someone is from facial appearance, and do not replace missing proof with a generated portrait.
3. Write the factual core: who the person or business helps, what it does and a supported proof point when available. Apply the maintained credibility rules: show a real moment rather than a trophy-name paragraph, and make relationship language no stronger than its evidence.
4. Create short and long variants without changing the underlying story. First person fits the individual’s own narrative; company “we,” a formal third-person bio or a clearly attributed quote may be appropriate. Do not force identical first lines or claim language across unlike entities.
5. Keep anonymous, domain-only or unverified praise on HOLD until evidence and permission meet the rule. Do not turn co-appearance into friendship, mentorship or endorsement. Derivative and repurposing work inherits the same evidence limits.
6. Store the approved source image and text versions in the project’s content library, then update each scoped profile through its supported source. Match actual crop and field limits; do not expose a private contact address to fill an optional public field.
7. Read back the public profiles and check intended asset, name and factual meaning. Document necessary differences and pending review states. An internal verification process or confidence label does not belong in public bio copy; keep a needed compliance disclosure when applicable.
8. Save the profile-by-profile change record, asset version and next owner. A missing account does not justify claiming every profile is updated. Route the exact remaining surface instead of asking for every login again.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] Each profile uses the right person/business identity and an approved sourced asset.
- [ ] Bio variants preserve verified facts, appropriate voice and limited relationship claims.
- [ ] HOLD evidence remains held across all derivatives; no fictional proof enters real copy.
- [ ] Public crops, fields and website links are checked for the declared profile set.
- [ ] The source library and unresolved profile owners are recorded without private contact or internal verification leakage.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example.** Lee Hart’s personal profile says “I help local owners explain roof repairs.” Oak Repair’s company Page says “We repair roofs for local homeowners.” The profile uses Lee’s approved headshot; the Page uses the company mark. Both tell a consistent factual story without pretending they are the same entity. A draft claim that Lee is a famous speaker’s mentor remains HOLD because the only evidence is an event appearance.
