# Upload Real Photos and Set Business Hours

People need to know what your business looks like and when it is open. This guide helps an owner fix the photos and hours on Google. Start with real photos and the hours your team can confirm.

**The path:** Real evidence → Upload and hours → Review state → Public check.

**Use this when:** a Business Profile has stale or missing owner media, changed operating hours or upcoming special dates.

## Inputs
- The exact Business Profile, legitimate editing access and actual verification state.
- Recent rights-cleared source photos with identity/context confirmed from records, plus logo and cover choices. The source’s ten-photo goal is a house target, not a reason to invent images.
- Owner-confirmed regular hours, breaks, service-specific hours where supported and known special dates; distinguish customer-facing hours from a separate phone service.

## First-run prompt

> Use the supplied real business media and confirmed hours to update the exact profile within scope. Check saved and public states separately. Preserve valid customer content, do not identify people by face, and do not promise Google will choose the requested cover first.

## Steps
1. Match photos to the actual business and location using the source record, permissions and context. Choose useful exterior, interior, team and at-work views where applicable. Do not identify people from faces or declare an image synthetic just from appearance.
2. Check the current provider format and quality requirements and prepare suitable files without misleading alterations. Use the actual logo and a cover that fairly represents the business. Record available real images; a count shortfall is a collection need, not license to add filler.
3. Upload through the supported profile controls and save media IDs or recognizable source references. Inspect pending, rejected and live states. An upload completion screen does not prove the public gallery changed.
4. Review outdated owner uploads in scope and remove only those whose retirement is intended. Customer photos use a different reporting process; an old or unflattering image is not automatically a policy violation. Do not flag valid customer media just to control the gallery.
5. Set regular hours to the actual customer-facing operation, including breaks where needed. Use the current Hours controls. A separate answering service does not make a closed storefront open 24 hours.
6. Set known special hours for relevant upcoming dates. Use supported More hours for an appropriate service with different availability; do not force a field that the business type does not support. Unknown holiday plans remain a named owner question, not guessed hours.
7. Read back the public hours on phone and desktop, and check the intended media are live. Google may choose a different first image despite the saved cover selection; record the observed display rather than repeatedly claiming control over it.
8. Save the source-media list, confirmed hours, evidence dates and remaining collection/review needs. Hand off real photo refreshes and future special dates under the project’s adopted maintenance plan without claiming a ranking benefit from a fixed upload count.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] Photos are real, sourced, rights-cleared and relevant to this business or location.
- [ ] Available count and upload/review/live states are truthful; no stock or invented filler is used.
- [ ] Regular, break and known special hours reflect the actual operation.
- [ ] Public display is checked without promising the selected cover always appears first.
- [ ] Customer media, private facts and any pending hour decisions are handled through the correct scope.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example.** Oak Repair has eight usable real photos, below the house target of ten. The lesson uploads those eight and requests two additional real at-work sources through the existing owner handoff. Saturday hours are 9–12, while the phone service continues later; the storefront is not labeled open all day. The selected cover is saved, but Maps shows another valid image first. That is recorded accurately, not counted as an upload failure.
