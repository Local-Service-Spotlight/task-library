# Create Facebook/Instagram campaigns with location targeting

You want more people in the area you serve to see a useful post. This guide helps you set up a small local ad test. Start with your service area and one post that has worked.

**The path:** Service area → proven post → saved test → delivery check.

**Use this when:** The business has a proven post, a defined local goal and authorized scope for a Meta campaign.

## Inputs
- Exact business portfolio, ad account, Page and Instagram identity with the roles needed for the planned action.
- Actual service area, current offer, verified content rights and original post ID.
- Goal and success target, account currency/time zone, billing owner, spend ceiling and dates; required tracking evidence for that goal.

## First-run prompt

> Prepare this Facebook/Instagram test using the existing approved post and service area. Check the identity, goal, current audience controls, budget type and end date. Launch only within the supplied authority, then re-read the saved settings and delivery. Report any unsupported minimum or location requirement without broadening it.

## Steps
1. Read the agreed [Goals, Content, and Targeting](https://blitzmetrics.com/gct-business-strategy/) and prerequisite record. Confirm the offer is real, customer proof is usable and the post already earned relevant signal. Paid distribution is not the repair for an unproven offer.
2. Open the exact authorized account and confirm which Page/Instagram identity will appear. Reuse existing valid assets; do not create a duplicate account or transfer ownership to solve a role gap.
3. Choose the objective that measures the stated job. An on-platform engagement test needs its own platform metric; a website-lead test also needs a checked destination and the required conversion path. Do not install every tracker for every goal.
4. Enter the supported location controls for the real service area and check any special-category restrictions. Do not default to guessed ages, home ownership or every available interest. Read expansion/suggestion labels before claiming a tight local audience.
5. Use the original eligible post, then inspect each chosen placement with sound off. Check captions, crops, identity, claims, destination and mobile usability. Save a distinct creative only if the authorized plan calls for it and note the new ID.
6. Set the method’s small seven-day test where accepted. Record ad-set versus campaign budget control, account minimums, budget pacing and actual total limits. Calculate all concurrent commitments; $1/day is not a promise that every day bills exactly $1.
7. Re-read the final summary before an authorized launch, including currency, dates and budget. After saving, record actual IDs and status. A draft or approved ad without impressions is not verified delivery.
8. Check spend, delivery, link and location-quality evidence early. Protect the existing ceiling and correct harmful errors within scope. At day seven, score against the original target and route the next action rather than extending the end date by default.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example:** A shop serves Cedar Town and wants local people to watch a repair tip. Its real service boundary is recorded before settings are chosen. The preview shows the same post on both chosen placements with legible captions. A seven-day test is staged at $1/day where supported, with the account’s spend controls documented. If the location option includes people only recently in the area, that limit stays in the log. The report checks actual inquiries for service fit rather than promising every viewer lives nearby.
