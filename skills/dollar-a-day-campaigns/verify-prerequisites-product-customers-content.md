# Verify prerequisites (product, customers, content)

Ads help more people see what your business does. Before you spend, check that the offer helps people and that you have proof to share. This guide helps you find what is ready and what needs work.

**The path:** Real offer → customer proof → useful content → honest decision.

**Use this when:** Paid amplification is proposed for a business or offer, or a previous prerequisite gap has been repaired.

## Inputs
- Permitted evidence of real sales, delivery, customer response and unit economics; an actual business owner who can resolve missing facts.
- Source reviews/testimonials and reuse rights; recent organic posts and their available metrics.
- The proposed goal, audience and budget context; a tracker for the three findings and next owner.

## First-run prompt

> Check this offer, customer evidence and organic content before paid amplification. Link the evidence for each finding, mark missing facts unknown and give a clear ready, needs-work or hold decision. Do not create reviews, invent sales or treat a catalog status as permission to spend.

## Steps
1. Name the exact offer and buyer need. Read evidence of actual delivery and customer value, not only the owner’s pitch. A launch for a new offer needs that gap stated rather than borrowing another offer’s proof.
2. Check the product/service case with appropriate records: real purchases, fulfilled work, referrals or repeat use where relevant. A long-life service may not have repeat purchases within a short window; judge its actual buying cycle instead of forcing that sign.
3. Review the available margin and target acquisition cost with the business owner’s records. Keep gross revenue, margin and allowable ad cost separate. If the economics are not supplied, mark the financial premise unknown rather than inventing a target.
4. Collect at least three verifiable instances of positive customer experience for this source task’s proof check, when available. Verify the original review or testimonial and permission for reuse. Do not solicit only positive reviews, pay for praise or turn an anecdote into a fabricated endorsement.
5. Inspect the actual organic content and its useful signal. Use comparable recent posts, comments, shares and watch data. Strong content means evidence of relevance, not a polished video or a famous name alone.
6. Record each of the three prerequisites with linked evidence and a clear finding: ready, needs work, or unknown. A ready finding has a reason. If a needed premise is weak or unknown, the paid-launch step remains pending even though the inspection itself can be finished.
7. Route the exact gap: service/value to the operations owner, customer-proof verification to the evidence owner, or missing useful content to the content owner. Do not claim another skill will manufacture genuine customer delight.
8. When the required premises are supported, write the [Goals, Content, and Targeting](https://blitzmetrics.com/gct-business-strategy/) baseline and hand over to measurement/budget planning. Passing this inspection is not a spend authorization or proof that any campaign has run.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] The exact offer and business evidence support the product finding.
- [ ] Customer proof is verifiable, permissioned and not manufactured.
- [ ] Organic content has relevant source metrics or a stated gap.
- [ ] All three findings preserve unknowns and name the next owner.
- [ ] Readiness, budget authority and actual campaign outcome are separate states.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example:** A repair shop has real completed jobs, three verified customer reviews and a seat-fix clip with useful questions. Its owner has not supplied the margin or target lead cost. The report marks product delivery and customer proof as supported, and the paid-cost premise as unknown. The operator can finish this inspection and route the missing economics to the owner. It does not invent a target to pass the gate or claim the clip has already brought paid customers.

Run before a new paid-amplification plan and after meaningful offer or evidence changes. Recheck a prior gap when new proof arrives. This is not a mandatory new recurring schedule.
