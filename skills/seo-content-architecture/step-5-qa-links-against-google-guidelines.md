# Step 5: QA links against Google guidelines

Check that each new link helps readers reach the right page. This guide tests the words, the page they open and the reason to click. Start with the exact list of links that changed and their live pages.

**The path:** Exact changed links → Meaning and destination checks → Repairs or held rows → Accepted log

**Start when:** Step 4 has saved or released a defined set of links and supplied the actual change log.

## Inputs

- [Place the accepted links](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=step-4-create-links-with-proper-anchor-text-and-placement#task-step-4-create-links-with-proper-anchor-text-and-placement) log with source/target, anchor, surrounding text, exact revision and save/public state.
- The accepted [Goals, Content, Targeting: the result, source material and people served](https://blitzmetrics.com/gct-business-strategy/) and current owning target pages, plus normal public access to the changed source pages.
- The maintained [internal-linking method](https://blitzmetrics.com/internal-linking/) and current Google primary link guidance.

## Steps

1. Start from the exact changed-link log and read each link in its actual normal page context. Compare it with the saved source, not just a preview or cached screenshot. A stale public copy or inaccessible page is a specific verification gap; do not approve it because the CMS raw text is correct.
2. Check the anchor’s promise against the destination. It should be concise, descriptive and natural; the house three-to-six-word preference is not a Google word-count rule. A real two-word name can be correct, and repeating an accurate name is not automatically keyword stuffing.
3. Check that the surrounding sentence gives a useful reason to follow the link and remains factually true. Confirm the named person, company, method or offer matches. Owned explanatory links must not replace a necessary primary citation, sign-in or download destination with a general guide.
4. Inspect placement and link behavior. Ordinary crawlable links use real anchors with destinations, and readers must be able to see and use them. Contextual body links are this change set’s purpose; useful menu/footer navigation is not inherently manipulative. Hold hidden, misleading or unrelated link schemes.
5. Review volume by reader usefulness rather than a fixed links-per-word ratio. Remove redundant or forced additions where they add no value. Google states no ideal link count; a page with many relevant sources and a short page with few links need different judgments.
6. Follow each changed destination and record actual responses and redirect meaning. Correct a stale internal alias when the verified final target serves the same purpose; do not rewrite an intentional external evidence link blindly. Handle removed content with a relevant replacement or the proper reviewed removal behavior, never an arbitrary redirect.
7. Repair in-scope defects through the same maintained source rail and repeat only the affected checks. Save accepted, failed and held rows with exact evidence and owners, plus any wider issues discovered outside this scope. Pass the verified change set to measurement without declaring full-site compliance from a limited link review.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

Quality assurance (QA) means checking the actual result against its agreed requirements. Follow the [Article Guidelines](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/).

- [ ] Each accepted changed link has an accurate anchor, useful context, correct identity and actual destination check.
- [ ] The review distinguishes Google guidance from house preferences and finds no hidden or irrelevant link scheme in the changed set.
- [ ] Source/public mismatches, failed links and out-of-scope defects retain their real states and owners.
- [ ] The exact output, source revision, reviewer evidence and remaining owner action are saved.
- [ ] For any reader-facing output, the short grade-five opening states the reader’s useful outcome and supporting method or proof. The body delivers that promise; a useful authentic visual appears in the first screen. Retain exact text and quoted reviewer evidence.
