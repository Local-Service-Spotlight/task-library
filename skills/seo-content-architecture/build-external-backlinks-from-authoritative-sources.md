# Build external backlinks from authoritative sources

Help the right people find and check your work. This guide helps you earn useful links through real work, talks and source material. Start with proof you can share and sites that serve the same people.

**The path:** Real proof → Relevant publisher shortlist → Approved contribution → Verified placement

**Start when:** A person or business has verified public proof and an agreed topic for relevant outside coverage.

## Inputs

- The subject’s verified name, site and topic pages, plus source material cleared for public use.
- Current press, podcast and partner records, existing links, ownership and any paid relationships.
- [Choose the right entity destination](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=follow-entity-linking-decision-tree#task-follow-entity-linking-decision-tree) and the specific authority for pitches, submissions, public thanks or paid placements; research alone grants none.

## Steps

1. Inspect the existing link and proof inventory before seeking new placements. Identify the reader, useful claim and strongest actual evidence. Separate independent coverage, paid placements, self-published material and neutral appearances instead of treating every mention as an endorsement.
2. Build a small target list from relevant publications, podcast hosts, industry groups and real professional relationships. Read their work and contribution rules. Record why their audience would use the source material; a domain score alone does not establish relevance or Google’s trust.
3. Prepare a useful contribution such as original data, a sourced answer, an interview topic or a real case study. Tie it to the host’s actual audience and existing work. Do not create a testimonial, exchange links as a condition of praise, or promise results for a backlink.
4. Use [entity linking, choosing the right home for a named person or topic](https://blitzmetrics.com/entity-linking/) to propose the destination. A person’s name points to their verified home; a method explanation points to its owning guide. A story may cite a specific supporting source. Give the editor honest descriptive wording without requiring an unnatural keyword phrase.
5. Send a specific pitch or submission only under existing authority, using the publisher’s supported route. Record sent, accepted, scheduled, published and declined separately. The publisher decides whether to include a link; a booking or friendly reply is not a live placement.
6. When a placement is public, read it and follow the actual link. Save source URL, date, supported fact, anchor, target, redirect outcome and any sponsorship label. Paid links require appropriate qualification; paying for advertising is not the same as earning an independent endorsement.
7. Add relevant verified proof to the owning hub with context and attribution, strongest relevant evidence first. Prepare or send thanks under its separate authority. Pass dated link changes and comparable search or referral measures to [Metrics, Analysis, Action: what happened, why and what to do](https://blitzmetrics.com/maa/) without claiming that one link caused a ranking change.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

Quality assurance (QA) means checking the actual result against its agreed requirements. Follow the [Article Guidelines](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/).

- [ ] Each proposed placement has genuine topic and audience fit, source proof and an honest target.
- [ ] Sent requests, booked appearances and verified public links have distinct states.
- [ ] Accepted proof is attributed and relevant; payment and independence are not concealed.
- [ ] The exact output, source revision, reviewer evidence and remaining owner action are saved.
- [ ] For any reader-facing output, the short grade-five opening states the reader’s useful outcome and supporting method or proof. The body delivers that promise; a useful authentic visual appears in the first screen. Retain exact text and quoted reviewer evidence.
