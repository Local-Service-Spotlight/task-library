# Post roundup/listicle content

Your readers may need one place to find your best answers. This guide helps you turn strong past posts into a useful list. Start with one reader question and the pages that truly help answer it.

**The path:** Reader question → proven source pages → useful roundup → checked publication.

**Use this when:** A campaign or period has enough relevant published source material for a reader-focused roundup.

## Inputs
- Exact published source URLs and dated evidence of their usefulness; rights to images, clips and excerpts.
- The reader question, existing topic hub and [SEO Tree](https://blitzmetrics.com/seo-tree/) placement so the roundup has a distinct job.
- Current [Article Guidelines](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/), the actual CMS/save rail, author/category requirements, publication scope and any named distribution channels.

## First-run prompt

> Build a helpful roundup for this reader question from the supplied verified pages. Keep the topic hub authoritative, use meaningful owned links and check media rights. Publish only through the supported existing CMS rail when authorized, then verify the canonical page at desktop and phone sizes. Distribution and paid promotion must match their own existing scope.

## Steps
1. Choose the exact question the roundup answers and check existing pages first. Update an appropriate existing roundup when it serves the same job; do not create a competing definitive page or rename a source hub to make room.
2. Select the strongest relevant source pieces using the period’s actual evidence. The source suggests five to ten, but use only as many as the question warrants. Different metrics serve different goals; traffic alone does not prove qualified business impact.
3. For each item, read the full source and write a brief takeaway that helps the reader decide whether to open it. Link with descriptive text to the maintained owned leaf or guide. Preserve primary external proof when needed instead of replacing evidence with a vague internal link.
4. Place a clear grade-5 opening and a meaningful photo or diagram in the first desktop and phone screen. Each item needs useful visual context when it aids understanding, with accurate captions, alt text and rights. Keep video embeds silent during checks and captions available.
5. Tie the roundup to its parent branch and relevant included leaves through the [SEO Tree](https://blitzmetrics.com/seo-tree/). Explain methods on their maintained owned pages at first mention. Do not add every sibling or turn the roundup into a wall of unrelated links.
6. Review factual and relationship claims against their sources. A name, image or mention does not establish endorsement. Do not turn a selected example into a task recipe or certify an article merely because it appears in this roundup.
7. Use the actual supported CMS pipeline for the existing type and builder. Preserve canonical URL, Page/Post identity and durable save source. Apply the maintained editorial checks; plugin scores and automatic link suggestions do not replace semantic and visual review.
8. When authorized, publish and read back the saved source, then the ordinary canonical URL. Check links, readable first-screen content, media and phone/desktop overflow. Share only to named channels in scope, then measure the roundup’s own response before treating it as a paid winner.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] The reader question and distinct SEO Tree role are clear.
- [ ] Each selected source was read and linked with relevant descriptive text.
- [ ] Opening, meaningful lead visual, captions and claims meet current standards.
- [ ] Publication preserves the supported CMS type, source and canonical URL.
- [ ] Actual public read-back and channel receipts are separate from saved drafts and future boost eligibility.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example:** A shop has three strong articles on loose seats, brake checks and tire wear. It makes “Three checks before a school bike ride” as a short entry page, linking each takeaway to the full guide and linking up to the bike-care hub. A simple three-part inspection diagram leads the page. The site has an existing Page built with its supported builder, so the operator updates that source rather than converting it to a Post. The roundup’s own response is still unknown after publication; the success of its linked pieces does not automatically qualify it for a paid boost.
