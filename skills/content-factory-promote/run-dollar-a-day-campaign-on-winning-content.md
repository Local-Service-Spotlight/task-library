# Run Dollar a Day campaign on winning content

You have posts that people already find useful. This guide helps you test them with a small ad budget and learn from the results. Start with the proof, the goal and the total you can spend.

**The path:** Qualified content → priced tests → due verdicts → next bounded cycle.

**Use this when:** The owner has a supported offer, useful customer proof and qualified content for an authorized paid-amplification plan.

## Inputs
- The prerequisite finding, source posts with rights and organic metrics, and the goal/target.
- Exact platform/account, audience plan and measurement required for that goal; current roles and action scope.
- Currency, budget controls, total commitments, start/end dates, stop rules and existing test log/reviewer.

## First-run prompt

> Run this bounded campaign from the supplied qualified posts and goal. Reuse current verified setup, price every test and avoid duplicate active rows. Carry out only authorized actions, check early delivery and spend, and give each due row an evidence-based verdict. Record the actual result without inventing future winners.

## Steps
1. Review [verify prerequisites product customers content](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=verify-prerequisites-product-customers-content#task-verify-prerequisites-product-customers-content) and the actual goal. A catalog complete label is not a client-readiness pass or spend permission. Preserve unresolved business or proof gaps that affect this plan.
2. Select qualified original posts through [identify signals worth amplifying](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=identify-signals-worth-amplifying#task-identify-signals-worth-amplifying). Recheck rights, claims, captions and current paid eligibility. Do not use a new derivative’s inherited popularity as proof of its own performance.
3. Confirm only the tracking and account assets needed for the chosen goal. A view/engagement test and a website-sale test have different requirements. Reuse valid setup instead of demanding every analytics system be installed first.
4. Build the budget-supported audience/creative rows. The core method may layer one post per day; simultaneous tests are optional. Record each goal, amount, dates and due reviewer. Use the correct budget level for the chosen platform.
5. Price the nominal $1/day for seven days where supported, plus all overlapping commitments and relevant fees. Verify actual spend controls and platform minimums. A 20-row nominal week is $140, not a single $7 test or a zero-risk commitment.
6. Before any authorized launch, read the final settings and current shared log. After saving, record IDs/status and check early delivery, links and spending. Avoid unnecessary edits while retaining existing protective-stop authority.
7. At each full-window due date, score the matching cost and useful result. Stop failures under the agreed rule; hold inadequate evidence with a date and spending decision. About 90% retirement is not a forced quota.
8. For a qualified winner, consider the current $30 total/30-day continuation or a separately scoped increase at no more than 2× per step. New audiences and nurture stages require their own inputs. Close the cycle with a [Metrics, Analysis, and Action](https://blitzmetrics.com/maa/) report; repeat only under a real existing arrangement.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] Offer/proof and content qualify without invented readiness or rights.
- [ ] The goal’s actual measurement and audience conditions are checked.
- [ ] Every commitment fits the real budget controls and end dates.
- [ ] Early safety checks and full-window verdicts are both recorded.
- [ ] Next cycles, increases and overlapping recipe counts do not happen automatically.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example:** A shop has two qualified clips and $14 for their nominal seven-day tests in an eligible account. The shared log shows one clip already has a staged test, so the campaign completes that row and adds only the missing one. At review, one has useful evidence and one does not. The report pauses the weak row within scope and proposes a $30 total/30-day continuation for the qualified one. That proposal is not labeled applied, and no new campaign is counted merely because a second guide helped review the same run.

Keep any unsupplied destination or new action outside that scope clearly pending.

Save source IDs, versions, decisions, checked outputs and next owner in the project tracker. Before a retry, check the saved state and other workers’ changes. Keep agent media muted with volume zero before playback. If mute cannot be verified, use captions, metadata or still frames. State the limit: silent visual checks do not prove spoken-word accuracy or audio quality. Do not start sound through the user’s speakers unless explicitly asked.
