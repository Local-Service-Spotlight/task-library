# Engage with social comments

People may ask good questions under your posts. This guide helps you reply with care and keep track of what needs more help. Start with the full thread so your answer fits what the person asked.

**The path:** Real thread → factual reply → scoped action → topic or handoff.

**Use this when:** An authorized comment review is due or a specific comment needs attention on a channel in scope.

## Inputs
- Access to the exact approved channels/posts and their complete comment threads, including existing replies.
- The owner’s factual voice guidance, approved offer/support information and scope for replies or moderation.
- Current support/escalation owner, moderation rules and a tracker for unresolved comments, useful questions and possible proof reuse.

## First-run prompt

> Review these exact comment threads, check for existing replies and prepare or post only responses already in scope. Answer from verified facts and the owner’s permitted voice. Keep private details out of public replies, preserve honest criticism and route unresolved issues to the real owner. Log questions without turning praise into an unapproved testimonial.

## Steps
1. Review only the named channels and recent/boosted posts in scope. Read the whole thread and check whether another worker already answered. Do not assume the source’s historical named group or every social account belongs in this job.
2. Classify each item: answerable question, useful feedback, support issue, abuse/spam or no reply needed. The source’s daily review and 24-hour response aim can guide an agreed service standard, but not every short comment needs an artificial question in return.
3. Use verified facts to answer the actual question in the owner’s permitted voice. Do not invent first-person experience, prices, delivery promises or relationships. A needed follow-up question should resolve the person’s need, not prolong the thread solely for engagement.
4. Post within existing reply authority and read back the exact comment. If approval or a source fact is genuinely missing, save the draft and name that narrow gap; do not demand approval again for already authorized routine replies.
5. Handle support details through the approved path without exposing customer records or requesting sensitive data publicly. Preserve honest criticism. Apply hiding/reporting/removal only under the actual moderation rule and authority, and record the reason.
6. Like or pin only when supported by the channel and appropriate within scope. These actions are not mandatory proof of a successful reply. Do not claim comment activity guarantees lower ad costs.
7. Capture recurring questions as topic ideas with source links. Treat praise as possible evidence requiring permission/context for reuse; a screenshot of a comment is not blanket permission to publish a testimonial everywhere.
8. Log what was answered, what remains open, response timing and the real next owner. Keep observed replies, referrals and ad metrics separate; do not attribute cost changes to the conversation without supporting evidence.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] Every reviewed item has the correct thread and duplicate check.
- [ ] Replies are factual, useful and within the owner’s existing voice/action scope.
- [ ] Private details and honest criticism are handled under the real support/moderation policy.
- [ ] Posted actions have read-back; unanswered items have a reason and next owner.
- [ ] Topic/proof ideas remain distinct from approved reuse and claimed paid results.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example:** A viewer asks whether the seat-fix tip applies to a carbon frame. The operator checks the approved technical source and replies that the frame maker’s torque guidance matters; no guessed number is supplied. A second comment reports a missed appointment, so the reply uses the existing support route without exposing the booking details. A third says “great tip” and needs no forced question. The log records one new video topic and a support handoff, not a guaranteed drop in ad cost.
