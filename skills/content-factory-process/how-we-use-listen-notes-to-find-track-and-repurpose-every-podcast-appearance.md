# How we use Listen Notes to find, track, and repurpose every podcast appearance

**Use this when** the master podcast inventory needs the broad RSS, show-notes, metadata, and transcript discovery that Listen Notes provides.

## Inputs
- The person's canonical name and every known public name variation
- Known podcasts the person hosts or has appeared on
- The master podcast inventory or an import sheet with a defined verification-status field

## Tools
- Listen Notes in a browser; basic search does not require an account, while export, alerts, or other features may require current platform access
- Google Sheets or another tabular inventory tool
- Podchaser, YouTube, and browser search for cross-checking
- A browser capture tool when the work report requires screenshots

## Steps
1. Prepare a query list containing the person's exact quoted name, each exact quoted name variation, and known show titles. Record the query used and the search date so another operator can reproduce the result.
2. Search one quoted name at a time on Listen Notes and open the `Episodes` tab. Record the total result count for each query; do not treat that count as the number of verified appearances.
3. Export the episode results to CSV when the feature is available. Otherwise record podcast name, episode title, publish date, Listen Notes URL, source episode link, and the matching title, description, or transcript context manually.
4. Open the `Podcasts` tab and identify shows the person hosts or that use the person's name in their show metadata. Keep hosted shows distinct from guest appearances.
5. Sort Episodes by newest first and use language, region, genre, and publish-date filters only when they match the assignment. Record filters so relevant results are not silently excluded.
6. Review transcript-search snippets and show notes for identity and role. A name in a transcript can be a useful discovery lead, but a mention does not prove the person appeared; mark mention-only and ambiguous results accordingly.
7. Record Listen Score and percentile for the highest-ranked shows that contain verified appearances. Use those signals to prioritize review and repurposing, not as proof that an appearance is genuine.
8. For an already verified priority episode, the Listen Notes Clip tool may be used to create a shareable excerpt from a documented start and end time. Record the clip link in the inventory; do not clip an unverified or rights-restricted episode.
9. Merge the Listen Notes export into the master inventory. Deduplicate against Podchaser and YouTube by show, episode title, date, and source URL while preserving every platform link on the surviving record.
10. Cross-reference unmatched Listen Notes rows against Podchaser and YouTube, and cross-reference those sources back against Listen Notes. Keep unresolved rows in a review queue rather than promoting them to confirmed.
11. If the account and authorization permit, subscribe to the name query by RSS or email. Route every new alert through the same verification and deduplication steps before adding it to the inventory.
12. Hand verified priority rows to the Content Factory for transcription, articles, clips, and promotion. The Listen Notes search is complete only when its results are tracked in the master inventory, not when the search page has been viewed.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)
- [ ] Every canonical name variation was searched in quotes and each result count was recorded
- [ ] Episodes results were exported or manually captured with enough source data to verify them
- [ ] The Podcasts tab was checked and hosted shows were separated from guest appearances
- [ ] Results were sorted by date and every applied filter was documented
- [ ] Transcript/show-notes matches were reviewed; mentions and ambiguous identities are not labeled as appearances
- [ ] Listen Score and percentile are recorded for the highest-priority verified shows
- [ ] Listen Notes results were cross-referenced and deduplicated against Podchaser and YouTube
- [ ] Verified rows were added to the master inventory with source links and status
- [ ] An RSS/email alert is active when authorized and available, or the access blocker is recorded
- [ ] Priority verified episodes are queued for the appropriate Content Factory outputs
- [ ] Linked back to the definitive article and relevant siblings

- The live SOP records 477 Listen Notes results for Dennis Yu and explains how transcript indexing found name mentions that title-only searches would miss. Cross-referencing other sources also showed why those results still required identity checks and deduplication.
