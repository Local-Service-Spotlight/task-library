# How to inventory every podcast you've been on

**Use this when** a person has made podcast appearances over time but has no complete, verified record of where those episodes live or which ones deserve repurposing first.

## Inputs
- The person's canonical full name, alternate spellings, and former or shortened names used publicly
- Known shows the person hosts or has appeared on, plus known hosts, companies, and recurring topics for identity checks
- The personal-brand website and any supplied appearance lists, archives, or prior inventories
- Authorization and owner access if a public creator profile will be claimed or edited

## Tools
- Google Sheets for the master inventory
- Listen Notes, Podchaser, YouTube, Apple Podcasts or other directories surfaced by a verified result
- Browser search and show websites/show-notes pages
- ChatGPT or Claude with web search for discovery leads, followed by source verification

## Steps
1. Create one master inventory. At minimum record episode title, show, host, publish date, platform, canonical episode URL, the person's role, audio/video availability, engagement when visible, discovery source, verification status, and repurposing status.
2. Search Listen Notes for every quoted name variation and known show title. Record each query and result count, export the Episodes results when available, and identify any shows the person hosts from the Podcasts tab.
3. Search Podchaser for the person and known shows. If a creator profile exists, review its confirmed and suggested credits; if claiming or verification is required, do it only with the person's authorization and account access. A transcript mention is not a guest credit.
4. Search YouTube globally for each quoted name plus `podcast`, `interview`, and `episode`. Capture video-format appearances, conference conversations, streams, and host-channel uploads that audio directories may miss.
5. Search the open web and the person's site for the quoted name plus podcast and show terms. Check host websites and show notes, then search only the internal records supplied for this task. Treat AI results as leads and keep the underlying episode or show URL as evidence.
6. Merge all sources into the master sheet. Match duplicates by show, title, date, host, and episode URL; consolidate platform-specific copies into one appearance record with multiple platform links. Do not count a re-upload or directory mirror as a new appearance.
7. Verify identity and role for every row. Confirm that the person actually hosted, co-hosted, or appeared in the episode; mark mere mentions and same-name collisions as rejected, and leave ambiguous rows in a human-review queue.
8. Classify each verified appearance as video available or audio-only. Record comparable engagement signals where the source exposes them, then rank the top 20% for immediate repurposing; do not fabricate a ranking where metrics are unavailable.
9. Route priority episodes into the Content Factory: obtain the permitted source media, transcribe it, create articles and short clips, extract quote graphics, and consider Dollar-a-Day promotion only after organic performance identifies a winner. Keep every derivative linked to the source episode and canonical hub.
10. Turn hosts and recurring guests into a relationship list for follow-up, cross-promotion, and collaborative content. Set a recurring discovery alert where supported and append new verified appearances to the same inventory rather than starting over.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)
- [ ] Listen Notes was searched for all name variations and known show titles
- [ ] Podchaser was searched; an existing creator profile and its credit state were documented without bulk-confirming suggestions
- [ ] YouTube was searched for video appearances not indexed on audio platforms
- [ ] Host/show websites, the person's site, and supplied records were checked for missing episodes
- [ ] Every verified appearance records title, date, platform links, host/show, role, and evidence
- [ ] Cross-platform copies are merged and duplicate appearance records equal zero
- [ ] Mentions, same-name collisions, and unresolved leads are separated from confirmed appearances
- [ ] Audio-only versus video availability is flagged for repurposing decisions
- [ ] The top 20% with supported engagement evidence is marked for priority repurposing
- [ ] New-appearance monitoring and a continuing update owner/cadence are documented
- [ ] Linked back to the definitive article and relevant siblings
