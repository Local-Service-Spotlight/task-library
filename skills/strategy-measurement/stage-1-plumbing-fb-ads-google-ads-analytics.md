# Stage 1: Plumbing (FB Ads, Google Ads, Analytics)

Make sure your business can see what its marketing does. This guide checks the accounts and tracking before work goes live. Start with the right business, its allowed scope and the access list.

**The path:** Accepted scope → Asset ownership → Tracking tests → Readiness handoff

**Start when:** An accepted business scope is ready for the first Social Amplification Engine phase, or its tracking needs repair.

## Inputs

- [Social Amplification Engine, the six-phase marketing plan](https://blitzmetrics.com/social-amplification/) accepted scope and the authoritative client-status record.
- The current access register and asset IDs; business owners for Meta, Google Ads, website, Tag Manager, Analytics and Search Console as applicable to the agreed engine gate.
- [Digital Plumbing](https://blitzmetrics.com/digital-plumbing/) and the intended conversion definitions, test limits and change authority.

## Steps

1. Confirm the right business and relationship status first. Active Client or a separately scoped Special Project can supply the work path; Not Active or HOLD stops dependent operations. Read the current engine gate rather than treating a self-report as onboarding approval.
2. Inventory existing assets and ownership. Record the actual domain, account/property/container IDs and granted roles without copying secrets. Reuse working business-owned assets instead of creating duplicates.
3. Check Meta business and ad-account ownership and the required partner access through the account’s supported controls. Verify a permitted read; a displayed account name does not prove administrative access or correct ownership.
4. If Google Ads manager linking is in scope, use [Google’s current manager-link instructions](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7456530?hl=en). A link preserves the client account and does not automatically transfer administrative ownership or billing.
5. Validate the site’s actual analytics and tag setup against the agreed key events. Inspect existing tags before installing anything. Use [Google’s DebugView procedure](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/7201382?hl=en) for a controlled test device, with its consent state and event parameters; do not generate a real purchase or contact a customer to simulate proof.
6. Check each required destination for missing or duplicate test events and the correct account. Debug visibility demonstrates receipt, not full revenue attribution. Keep test traffic identifiable and remove temporary debug configuration as the official guide requires.
7. Save ownership, access and test receipts. The current engine requires Search Console, CMS, Analytics and Tag Manager green before shipping or amplification. Drafting can continue where allowed, but any missing required row keeps the release phase blocked; hand a precise repair to its owner.
