## Steps
1. Gather this cycle’s evidence.
2. Update the tracker’s actual project state.
3. Record metrics and analysis.
4. Assign next actions.
5. Review the record with the team and client.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

**Expected result:** A current Success Tracker that makes the relationship’s work, evidence and next actions understandable.

- [ ] Every completed item links a dated artifact
- [ ] Every metric names its source and period
- [ ] Every action has one owner and due date
- [ ] Unknown, blocked and not-connected states remain visible
- [ ] Record the exact source revision, actual result and evidence; retain failure, partial, blocked and unknown states.
- [ ] Write the execution's meta article and link the canonical task. Publication is a separate action under the current authority.
