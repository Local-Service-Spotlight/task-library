# Collect verified weekly measurements

See which work brings leads and sales to your business. This guide helps your team check the numbers before it chooses what to change. Start with the source and date range for each number.

**The path:** Source contract → Matching exports → Checked numbers → Weekly report

**Start when:** The agreed measurement window has closed for an authorized business or funnel.

## Inputs

- The accepted measurement source contract, business scope, period, timezone and comparison period.
- Read access or named exports from the sources that own the requested metrics: Search Console, site analytics, calls, forms, customer records, bookings and revenue.
- [Goals, Content, Targeting: the result, proof and people for this work](https://blitzmetrics.com/gct-business-strategy/) targets and the preceding report; the current [Measurement & Analytics procedure](https://localservicespotlight.com/skills-measurement-analytics/).

## Steps

1. Identify the authoritative source and definition for each metric. Search visibility, site visits, inquiries, qualified leads, bookings and collected revenue are different stages; do not substitute one for another.
2. Confirm the in-scope relationship and approved source access. Record which sources were actually read. Use not connected or unknown for inaccessible data, never zero.
3. Pull the current and preceding comparable windows. Retain export date, period, timezone, units, filters and attribution settings. Deduplicate leads by the client’s accepted identity rules while preserving source totals.
4. Compute ratios with named numerators and denominators. For the source’s low-ticket funnel, opt-in rate is opt-ins divided by landing visits, take-rate is buyers divided by its specified eligible audience, cost per lead is spend divided by ad-page opt-ins, and cost per buyer is spend divided by front-end buyers. Confirm the actual source denominator before computing take-rate; do not infer it from an unlabeled percentage.
5. Use the source’s 25% opt-in and 3% take-rate targets only for the applicable agreed funnel. Do not judge its page-rate thresholds below 100 visits. Preserve the source’s comparison notes for changes above 30%; neither a threshold nor a week-over-week move proves a cause.
6. If using front-end revenue divided by ad spend, label that ratio precisely. A ratio of at least one covers ad spend on that revenue basis; fees, fulfillment, refunds, margin and timing still matter. It is not proof of free leads, profit or permission to scale.
7. Reconcile totals and note missing sources, incomplete attribution and sample limits. Save the report, weakest supported stage and proposed next check. Hand findings to analysis and the relevant action owner; this measurement task changes no campaigns or funnels.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

Quality assurance (QA) means checking the result against its agreed requirements; see the [Article Guidelines](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/).

- [ ] Each value traces to its source, definition, unit and period; missing values remain unknown.
- [ ] Funnel thresholds apply only to the declared source contract and sufficient sample; zero denominators have no invented rate.
- [ ] Qualified leads, bookings and revenue stay separate; report and handoff are saved.
