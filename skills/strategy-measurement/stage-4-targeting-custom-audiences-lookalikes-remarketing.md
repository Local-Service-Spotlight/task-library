# Stage 4: Targeting (custom audiences, lookalikes, remarketing)

Help the right people see content that fits their needs. This guide helps you plan who should see each piece of work. Start with the real offer, service area and the data you are allowed to use.

**The path:** Customer facts → Audience hypotheses → Supported controls → Checked map

**Start when:** Goals and available content are clear, and the engine needs its targeting plan or an authorized audience setup.

- [The accepted goal and customer brief](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=stage-2-goals-mission-90-day-goals-cpa-roas-budget#task-stage-2-goals-mission-90-day-goals-cpa-roas-budget) and [available source content](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=stage-3-content-endorsements-why-video-3x3-grid-library#task-stage-3-content-endorsements-why-video-3x3-grid-library).

## Steps

1. Define the intended customer, problem, location, exclusions and campaign objective. Keep targeting last in the Goals, Content, Targeting sequence; do not choose a convenient list and invent a strategy around it.
2. Inventory permissible audience sources. Existing customer lists, site visitors, video viewers and interests are possible inputs, not mandatory data to upload. Record source, recency, rights and whether it is actually available.
3. Choose bounded audience hypotheses matched to the content and funnel job. Do not label every customer hot or every website visitor ready to buy; the source behavior and intended action determine the use.
4. Read the current account controls and official help before setup. [Meta Blueprint’s audience course](https://www.facebookblueprint.com/student/path/253166-advantage-plus-audiences) covers audience selection, detailed, custom and lookalike audiences. The account must establish which settings are firm controls and which are suggestions or expanded delivery; do not assume an old interest stack or 1% lookalike rule is universally available.
5. Prepare the exact source definition, retention window, inclusion/exclusion logic, country/service area and name for each audience. Check minimum eligibility and supported options in the actual interface. A too-small or unavailable pool stays a gap.
6. Create or edit only the audiences covered by existing authority using the supported interface. Verify saved definitions and status. An accepted file or saved audience is not proof of matching quality, actual reach or a launched ad.
7. Save the map with audience IDs, definitions, content fit and unresolved constraints. Hand it to amplification for a separate launch review, retaining no unnecessary personal data in public documentation.
