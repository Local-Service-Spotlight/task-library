# Analyze why underperformers fail

Find out why an ad or page is not doing its job before you change it. This guide helps your team choose one fix that the facts support. Start with the weak results and the goal set before the test.

**The path:** Goal + data → Find the weak step → Test the cause → Fix proposal

**Start when:** A measured item missed its agreed target and needs a diagnosis before a change is proposed.

## Inputs

- [Show what works and what needs review](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=show-whats-working-and-what-isnt#task-show-whats-working-and-what-isnt) with item IDs, periods, targets and sample limits.
- [Goals, Content, Targeting: the result, proof and people for this work](https://blitzmetrics.com/gct-business-strategy/) brief from launch, actual content, audience settings and landing path.
- Funnel data and source records for delivery, clicks, visits, qualified leads and sales; previous changes and conversion delays.

## Steps

1. Check tracking and the evaluation window first. Missing tags, an unread source, a changed definition or unfinished conversion delay can explain a weak number. Keep the case unresolved until those checks permit a verdict.
2. Locate the weak step: were the intended people reached, did they engage or visit, and did visits become the agreed result? Compare like campaigns and periods; one ratio does not establish a cause.
3. Inspect creative, meaning the message, image or video. A weak hook or low watch time is a hypothesis. Compare its content and delivery with a relevant stronger item before blaming the message.
4. Inspect targeting: read actual geography and audience settings, the queries or comments, and eligible customer characteristics. An ad name alone does not prove who saw it.
5. Inspect the offer and follow-up path. Check message match, load/form function, price and proof, then lead handling. Strong clicks with few bookings can reflect tracking, an unclear offer or missed calls; separate those possibilities.
6. Name one best-supported primary constraint when evidence supports it, with source links and alternatives ruled out. Otherwise state unknown cause and the next discriminating check. Do not force every item into a proven creative, targeting or offer bucket.
7. Recommend one bounded fix, its expected signal, owner, test window and limit. Read previous attempts before recommending another retry or a pause; elapsed cycles alone do not justify a kill. Pass the diagnosis to the decision owner.
