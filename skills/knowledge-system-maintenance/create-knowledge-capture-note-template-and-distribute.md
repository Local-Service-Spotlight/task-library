# Create Knowledge Capture Note template and distribute

A lesson form helps only if your team can find and use it. This guide puts the form where the work happens. Start with the checked form, then try it with the people who need it.

**The path:** Checked form → Workflow links → Real trial → Adoption review

**Start when:** A checked lesson form exists and the intended team needs a usable rollout and adoption check.

## Inputs

- [The checked four-field form](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=provide-knowledge-capture-note-template#task-provide-knowledge-capture-note-template) and a real filled example.
- Shared storage, the team’s three capture workflows, and their owners.
- Authority for training, source updates and any team notice; an agreed adoption-review date.

## Steps

1. Inspect the existing form before creating another. It needs source, insight, destination and priority, short hints and a real sourced example. Use the authoring task if these are missing.
2. Confirm one canonical saved copy and the receiving team’s access. Keep a revision and point copies back to it.
3. Use [the workflow-integration task](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=add-knowledge-capture-to-existing-workflows#task-add-knowledge-capture-to-existing-workflows) to put the form at call wrap-up, campaign review and audit closeout. Preserve already working links and route process changes through review.
4. Run an agreed walkthrough with one genuine lesson. Show how to cite the source, state the insight, choose a destination and set priority. A sample demonstration is training, not a completed client task.
5. Over the first two weeks, inspect real opportunities to capture lessons, saved notes and their timestamps. Check both use and quality; no notes may mean no relevant work, inaccessible storage or a missed capture.
6. Record adoption as observed, partial or untested for each workflow. Route repeated problems to a reviewed form/workflow change and retain who owns the next check.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

[Quality assurance (QA)](https://localservicespotlight.com/article-guidelines/) means checking the work against the agreed result.

- [ ] Canonical form and example are accessible at all three in-scope capture points.
- [ ] A real walkthrough and its participants/output are recorded.
- [ ] The two-week review measures actual notes and opportunities, with blockers retained.
