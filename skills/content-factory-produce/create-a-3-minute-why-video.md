# Create a 3-minute WHY video

People may know what you sell but not why you care. This guide helps an owner tell the true story behind the work. Start with one event that shaped your choice, then explain what it means for the people you serve.

**The path:** True origin story → Three speaking notes → Short video → Editor.

**Use this when:** The owner’s purpose and business goals are clear but there is no useful video that explains them.

## Inputs
- The owner’s stated purpose, current offer, and agreed business goals. Use the real source document, not an agent’s guessed memory.
- A true origin story the owner is willing to share and the facts needed to check it.
- The owner, a phone, and a real place connected to the story, with permission to film there.
- The approved raw folder, tracker access, and an editor. Record any people or details that must stay private.

## First-run prompt

> Help me shape my true origin story into three speaking notes for a three-minute video. Use my goals, offer, and facts. Flag anything you cannot verify. Give me a plain opening, a review checklist, and a file handoff; do not invent my biography or claim the video is ready for an ad.

## Steps
1. Read the agreed goals and offer with the owner. Write one plain sentence about who the business helps and why the owner does this work. If the purpose is not yet clear, draft that sentence for the owner to resolve before filming.
2. Choose one true moment that shows the purpose. Identify what happened, what the owner learned, and what changed in the work. Remove facts that cannot be confirmed; do not add drama to make the story stronger.
3. Make three speaking notes: the origin moment; who the owner serves and what they believe; what that means for a customer today. Practice saying the notes aloud without reading a script.
4. Set the phone in a real location linked to the story. Check the picture and microphone input. Keep private details out of the frame. The person, rather than a logo or stock scene, carries this story.
5. Record a take of about three minutes. Make the purpose clear in the first 30 seconds, then tell the example. This length is the source task’s format; it is not proof that the video fits a future ad placement.
6. Review the facts and meaning. Can a new viewer explain who this helps and why the owner cares? Keep a natural take; redo a failed recording or false claim. Use silent agent checks and name any remaining sound-review need.
7. Name the file YYYY-MM-DD-brandname-why-video with a take suffix if needed. Upload it, confirm the saved link, and log the source facts, purpose, length, rights, and next editor.
8. Queue it for [Upload the source to Drive and Descript](https://local-service-spotlight.github.io/task-library/?task=step-1-upload-video-to-google-drive-and-descript#task-step-1-upload-video-to-google-drive-and-descript). In the [Topic Wheel](https://blitzmetrics.com/topic-wheel/), this story belongs in WHY on the outside; the offer remains WHAT at the center. An About-page or ad version is a later editorial choice.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] The purpose is plain within the first 30 seconds, and a true origin moment supports it.
- [ ] The story names who the business serves and explains a useful customer meaning.
- [ ] The take is roughly three minutes; any format exception is stated and accepted.
- [ ] The raw file, facts, permissions, and review state are saved and handed to an editor.
- [ ] No later ad eligibility, performance, or public use is assumed from making the video.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example — not a founder biography.** Alex, the made-up owner of Maple Cycle, uses these notes for a sample three-minute story:

| Part | Speaking note | What it teaches |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Opening | “I opened this shop so people can ask basic bike questions without feeling silly.” | Clear purpose |
| Origin | “When I first rode to work, I did not know how to ask for help at a repair shop.” | One specific moment, not a slogan |
| Customer meaning | “We explain the work and quote before you choose what to do.” | What the belief changes in the service |

The sample outline allows 30 seconds for the purpose, 90 for the story, and 60 for its meaning. Those are planning notes, not measured video times. A real recording must use the real owner’s story; never give Alex’s invented past to another person.

## When this runs

Make an initial version when the owner’s purpose is clear. Update when the true story, offer, or needed explanation changes. The source sets no routine replacement schedule.

## Run with an agent

The agent can prepare the plan, organize allowed files, and check the evidence it can actually access. A person records real stories, supplies permission, or resolves an account step when needed. Save the current state in the project files and tracker. A model name does not create memory, access, a scheduler, or permission to act.

For all agent media checks, keep the player muted and its volume at zero before playback. If that cannot be checked, use captions, metadata, or still frames. Do not claim sound quality was heard when it was not.
