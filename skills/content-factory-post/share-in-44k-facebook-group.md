# Share in 44K Facebook group

A group post should give people something they can use. This guide helps you share a lesson from a real article. Start by checking the right group and its current posting rules.

**The path:** Correct group → Useful lesson → Submit and check → Replies and evidence.

**Use this when:** The distribution plan names a specific group and the checked article is ready for that audience.

## Inputs
- The exact group URL/ID and current rules. “44K” is the legacy task name, not a verified current member count or enough information to identify the group.
- The checked article, source facts, real media, and identities of any members to mention.
- The authorized posting identity, actual ability to post in the group, and the job’s message/publication scope.
- The distribution tracker, client-notification state when required by the plan, and a named person for comments and measurement.

## First-run prompt

> Use the exact group and article in the plan. Check group rules, posting identity and duplicate history. Share the useful lesson already authorized, then record whether it is pending or live. Keep replies and later measurements truthful; do not assume group size, reach, or boost eligibility.

## Steps
1. Open the exact group and read its current rules. Verify that the planned identity can post and that links, media, and this kind of content are allowed. Group membership does not guarantee immediate posting rights.
2. Read recent group posts and the tracker for duplicates. If the plan requires client notice first, verify that notification rather than assuming it from a checklist neighbor.
3. Write a short lesson: the problem, one supported detail, and what another member can do with it. Link the full article only when the current group rules allow that link. If links are barred, use a useful native lesson when it still meets the approved distribution goal; otherwise hold the post and name the rule conflict. Do not force a number into the post when the source has none.
4. Add a relevant real photo or clip if allowed and useful. Add accurate photo alt text or usable video captions where the group composer supports them; otherwise record the accessibility limit. Mention only confirmed featured members when appropriate. Keep media previews muted; no invented banner result or testimonial belongs in the post.
5. Submit when the job already authorizes posting. Read the resulting state: published, waiting for admin approval, rejected, or failed. Record the post/submission reference. Do not resubmit a pending item to force it through.
6. When live, confirm the text, link, identity, and media in the group. Restricted group content may require a member view; do not claim anonymous public access or general visibility.
7. Have the comment owner review legitimate questions within the agreed response plan. The source suggests a 24-hour review and a 48-hour measurement. Record actual dates and visible reactions/comments/shares; if a metric is unavailable, write UNKNOWN.
8. Save useful questions with their context in the [Topic Wheel](https://blitzmetrics.com/topic-wheel/) queue. Group response can inform a later Page-post test, but does not turn a group post into an eligible boost or prove business results.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] The exact group, rules, posting identity and allowed content are checked.
- [ ] The post teaches a source-backed point and includes working allowed links/media.
- [ ] Submitted, pending moderation and live states are kept separate.
- [ ] The tracker has the real reference and any pending owner/action; no duplicate submission was created.
- [ ] Later comment/metric work is either evidenced or explicitly pending, with no assumed engagement.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example — no real group or post.** A sample community for shop owners receives this draft:

“A repair quote can be clear without pretending we know everything from a photo. Our sample method separates what we can see from what needs an in-person check. Here is the full walkthrough: `https://maplecycle.example/repair-quote/`. What part of a quote do your customers ask about most?”

If the group displays “Pending admin approval,” the tracker records that state and the submission time. It does not record “posted to 44,000 people.” Even a live post’s group size would not prove how many people saw it.

## When this runs

One group submission per agreed article. The proposed 24-hour reply review and 48-hour metric check need a real named owner or configured authorized timer; there is no automatic recurring posting job.

## First-run setup and continuity

Use the exact account, source, destination and authority recorded for the job. Carry out publication, messages or repairs already authorized once their required checks pass; do not ask for the same approval again. If an action is outside that scope, finish the authorized work and state the specific remaining need. A login, plugin, or task file does not itself grant new authority.

Keep the latest state, record IDs, revisions and next owner in the project tracker or files. Before a retry, check the current saved/sent/published item to avoid duplicates or overwriting another edit. A model has no guaranteed memory, scheduler or account access just because it is called persistent. A future check needs a configured timer or a named person.

Keep all agent media previews muted with volume zero before playback. If this cannot be verified, use captions, metadata or still frames and state what was not tested. No first-load autoplay is part of these page instructions.
