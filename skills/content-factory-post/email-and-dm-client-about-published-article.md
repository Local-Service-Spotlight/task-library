# Email and DM client about published article

Your client should not have to hunt for their new article. This guide helps you send the live link and one useful next step. First, check the page and the client’s real contact details.

**The path:** Live page → Right person → Email and short note → Saved follow-through.

**Use this when:** The article is publicly visible and the job includes telling the client it is live. A DM is a direct message in the chat channel you both use.

## Inputs
- The checked public article, title, source context, and any social posts that are actually live.
- The exact client email/thread and normal direct-message channel from the project record. Check identity rather than guessing from search order.
- Existing authority to send on those channels, the approved sending account, and the requested ask. Carry forward permission already given for the job.
- The tracker, current notification history, next owner, and any agreed follow-up cadence.

## First-run prompt

> Use the checked article and exact client thread I provide. Draft a short email and a message with one useful ask. Carry out sends already authorized for this job, then verify each sent copy and log it. Check existing messages before follow-up. Do not claim a send, share, reply, or ad launch without evidence.

## Steps
1. Open the canonical article as a visitor. Confirm the correct title, source, and visible content. A saved WordPress response alone does not prove it is live. Check each social link before including it.
2. Read the exact client thread and tracker. Confirm the recipient and current channel. Check whether another worker already sent this notification; do not create a duplicate.
3. Write an email with a clear subject such as “Your article is live: [title].” Include the article link, two short lines on the useful point, and one specific ask. State only distribution or promotion that actually happened.
4. Prepare a short note for the client’s normal direct-message channel. Link the article or the relevant social post and use a personal, factual sentence. Do not claim a screenshot was taken or praise received unless that is true.
5. Send the email and message when these sends are already in scope. If the job covers only a draft or one channel, finish that authorized work and label the task result draft or partial rather than sent. Do not request duplicate approval for an authorized send.
6. Read back the sent copy in each exact thread. Log date, channel, recipient, sent-message reference, links, and the ask. A composed draft or a send-button click without confirmation is not a sent receipt. A sent-folder copy proves dispatch, not delivery: record a bounce, failed DM, or rejected destination as failed or pending with its next owner.
7. Follow the project’s existing cadence. The source proposes one follow-up after 48 hours with no reply; use it only when that follow-up is part of the agreed job. Check for a real reply and another worker’s recent message before following up.
8. If a reply arrives, record it accurately. A positive private reply can inform a later [Thank You Machine](https://blitzmetrics.com/thank-you-machine/) task; it is not automatically public testimonial permission. Handoff or close the remaining follow-up with its true state.

## Definition of done (QA checklist)

- [ ] The canonical article and included social links are actually live.
- [ ] Each in-scope message is confirmed sent to the intended person and recorded once; draft-only, partial, bounced and failed-delivery states are not called a completed notification.
- [ ] The message gives accurate context and one clear ask, with no invented promotion or response.
- [ ] Follow-up has a real owner/state; no reply is recorded as no reply, not failure of the send.
- [ ] Any quoted response retains its privacy and use limits.

## Example(s)

**Fictional teaching example — no message was sent.** Maple Cycle’s sample article explains how to ask for a repair quote.

**Subject:** Your article is live: How to ask for a bike repair quote

“Hi Alex, your guide is live at `https://maplecycle.example/repair-quote/`. It explains which details help the shop prepare for a visit. Would you share it with the next customer who asks how quotes work?”

The sample DM is: “Your quote guide is ready to share: `https://maplecycle.example/repair-quote/`. The photo checklist is the part I think riders will use most.”

If only the email has a sent receipt, record “email sent; DM still pending.” A client who has not replied has not supplied a testimonial. The `.example` address is an invented teaching URL and must be replaced with the real checked page.

## When this runs

One notification per published article, with source-recommended same-day timing when feasible. Preserve the project’s actual cadence. A 48-hour follow-up needs an existing authorized timer or a named manual owner; this file cannot wake itself.

## First-run setup and continuity

Use the exact account, source, destination and authority recorded for the job. Carry out publication, messages or repairs already authorized once their required checks pass; do not ask for the same approval again. If an action is outside that scope, finish the authorized work and state the specific remaining need. A login, plugin, or task file does not itself grant new authority.

Keep the latest state, record IDs, revisions and next owner in the project tracker or files. Before a retry, check the current saved/sent/published item to avoid duplicates or overwriting another edit. A model has no guaranteed memory, scheduler or account access just because it is called persistent. A future check needs a configured timer or a named person.

Keep all agent media previews muted with volume zero before playback. If this cannot be verified, use captions, metadata or still frames and state what was not tested. No first-load autoplay is part of these page instructions.
